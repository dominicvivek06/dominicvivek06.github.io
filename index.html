Summary

I design the data and AI platforms that large organisations run on. Over 20 years, about ten of them as a data architect, I have worked in manufacturing, energy, oil and gas, transport, construction, housing, media and technology.

Today my focus is Azure Databricks and Microsoft Fabric: Lakehouse and data mesh design, MLOps, GenAI and agentic AI. I decide how a central Group and its Business Units share responsibility, so data stays owned by the people who understand it and the platform stays secure, governed and affordable.

I still write Python, PySpark and SQL. That lets me prove a design with a working reference implementation before a team commits to it, and it keeps my architecture practical for the engineers who build it.
